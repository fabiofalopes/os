## Findings (each with verdict)

**F1 — The signal layer windows on the WRONG timestamp; two parsing vectors unfrozen (surface 1). Verdict: AMEND (A1+A2), not fatal.** The note's headline claim — "the signal layer IS point-in-time … carries the SEC's official `filingDate`/`acceptanceDateTime`" — names both timestamps but windows `S(i,m)` on `filingDate`. `acceptanceDateTime` is the true public moment (live sample: AAPL 8-K `filingDate` 2026-04-30, accepted **20:30:41Z** after close; acceptance can lag filingDate by days on edge filings). A filing dated month m−1 but accepted in month m would enter the month-m signal before it was public — a within-month look-ahead the 1-month skip only mostly absorbs. The fix is free and uses the note's own cited field: **window by `acceptanceDateTime`** (month of), `filingDate` only as logged fallback when absent (effectively never in-window). Two parsing vectors the executor would otherwise improvise: **(a)** live-verified `8-K/A` is a separate form string — "Form = 8-K only" must be the frozen literal `form == "8-K"` (`startswith` would silently include amendments; exclusion is conservative information loss, never look-ahead); **(b)** `items` missing/empty → frozen as excluded (can't verify materiality) + logged, never silently included. **Why not fatal:** monthly granularity + 1-month skip already absorb most leakage; all fixes tighten, none touch the verdict table.

**F2 — "Point-in-time" conflates timestamps with coverage (surface 1). Verdict: AMEND (inline relabel under A1).** The *texts* are point-in-time; the *cross-section of available filings* is NOT survivorship-free — and the bias bites exactly where this signal lives: Items 2.05/2.06/4.02 (exit/impairment/non-reliance) filings of names that died or delisted 2015–2026 are absent from the current-constituent universe. Direction: second-order for the long book (cross-sectional rank; the missing names populate the *bottom* of the S-distribution, the long quantile is barely touched), larger for the EW benchmark (survivorship inflates EW returns → *deflates* `SR_X` → conservative, same net direction as the inherited pilot-01 analysis). Fix: relabel "signal layer IS point-in-time" → "point-in-time **in timestamp** (immutable texts, official acceptance stamps), **NOT survivorship-free in coverage** (distress-item channel)," with a clear-KILL-still-conservative reading. The note's directional claim survives; its wording didn't.

**F3 — Rung-0 bar is fair; the luck-inflation attack is empirically mild (surface 2). Verdict: PASS + AMEND (A8, reporting only).** The sharpest fairness attack: +1.377 is the OOS number of a config KILLED at PBO = 0.77 — per the RESULT's own words "indistinguishable from selection luck at T = 42" — so the new signal could be asked to beat noise, and a genuine +1.2 signal would read NO-EVIDENCE. **Tested, not wondered:** the 18-config family mean OOS `SR_X`(EW) = **1.363** — the bar (1.377) sits at the family mean, not the ceiling (+1.634); the whole family posted +0.92→+1.63, so +1.377 is representative, not cherry-picked-high. Residual window-luck (2023–26 was kind to selection strategies) is **common-mode**: the new signal is judged on the identical window/universe/price bytes (re-verified above), so shared luck cancels in the comparison — this is the reuse-as-asset the note claims, and it holds. A8 (tightens reporting, moves nothing): RESULT must also report `SR_X` vs the family mean (1.363, cited) as diagnostic and explicitly reconcile against the luck-inflation question.

**F4 — Mechanical defects: lookback buffer truncates train; grid shrink clears (surface 2). **F5 — The ρ probe: one PROMOTE-gaming vector, one KILL-direction overclaim (surface 3). Verdict: AMEND (A4+A5).** **(a) Gameable as written:** the masking *procedure* is unspecified — an under-masked implementation (identity leaking through "headquartered in Cupertino," subsidiary brands, industry phrasing) keeps ρ high for a pure-memory signal, so the `ρ ≥ 0.8` PROMOTE gate can be passed by sloppiness. The KILL-row parenthetical ("→ memory/identity, not text extraction") is softened to "identity-sensitive (memory OR scale-context dependence)" in the caveats/amendment only — **the verdict table is not touched**; conservative-KILL is acceptable house style.

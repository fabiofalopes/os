## Hypothesis
utils/hooks.ts contains a hidden secondary LLM (Haiku) judging every tool call with two rule tiers.

## Method
Grep hooks.ts + permission paths for haiku/yolo/classifier; enumerate getSmallFastModel() usages tree-wide; read hook executor structure.

## Expected vs Observed
EXPECTED hidden judge in utils/hooks.ts. OBSERVED:
- ZERO matches for haiku|yolo|classifier in utils/hooks.ts (5,022 lines).
- hooks.ts = USER-configurable hook executor (executePreToolHooks/PostToolHooks/Stop etc.) — no LLM decision layer.
- getSmallFastModel() consumers: quota probe, awaySummary, tokenEstimation, WebSearchTool, agenticSessionSearch, AND utils/hooks/execPromptHook.ts:79 + execAgentHook.ts:118 — where model = hook.model ?? getSmallFastModel(), i.e., a user-defined hook MAY call a small model. No built-in hidden tool-call judge found anywhere in the permission path.

## Verdict
Claim NOT REPRODUCED in held v2.1.88. Downgrade CLM-0013 to grade 2 (claimed, single derived source) with pointer corrected. Plausible origin of misread: execPromptHook small-model calls OR later-version feature. Re-test ONLY against a version-specific artifact.

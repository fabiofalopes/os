# The Forge Synthesis — One Mind in Six Parts

> The six 2026-07-20 planning notes are not six ideas; they are one system seen from six angles. **The Forge is a self-compounding mind: a cron swarm of specialized roles runs daily on what already exists and what is carefully fetched, turning sessions into durable knowledge and validated skills, under the law that nothing is promoted unless it survives falsification — all to climb a staged arc from mapped child-brain to self-funding entity.** This page is the single entry point; each link below is the deep version of one part.

## 1. The arc — where it's going ([[Bootstrap to Self-Funding — The Agent Life Arc]])
`map → prove-one-thing → earn-a-little → cover-own-cost → compound`. Each stage has an explicit gate; no stage spends money it hasn't reasoned about. Value hypotheses live in a ranked ledger (`wiki/value/ledger.md`), not in vibes. Human sovereignty over capital never relaxes — autonomy in *work*, never in *spending*. We are in **Stage 0** now: mapping the child brain.

## 2. The engine — how it moves ([[Daily Cron Sessions — Swarm Harness Master Plan]])
One brain, three clocks: **fleet-optimizer** is the heartbeat (hourly infra health), **Forge tiers 0/1** are the metabolism (capture → consolidate, cheap models), **SkillOpt** is the learning (nightly, validation-gated skill forging). The compounding loop: sessions → digests → wiki/MEMORY, and sessions → skill candidates → `best_skill.md` → deployed into next day's workers. The shaping constraint: run *as much as possible* without burning tokens — night-rate Qwen (0.2×, 22:00–08:00), per-role caps, gates instead of retries.

## 3. The cast — who does the work ([[Agent Roles & Orchestrator — The Moat]])
Different personalities for different work: Scout, Scribe, Curator, Janitor, Distiller, Smith, Quant, Steward — each with its own prompt, tool allowlist, model tier, budget, and validation gate. The **Conductor** composes them under budget; the **Critic** is an adversarial lens invoked before any promotion. The moat is not any single role but the orchestration layer + the shared substrate (one vault, MEMORY.md, LOG.md, INDEX.md, one skill store) that makes the cast *one mind*. Instances are ephemeral; the entity persists through the substrate getting richer.

## 4. The method — what counts ([[Operating Principle — Test Don't Wonder]])
The epistemic constitution: (1) claim → evidence, always; (2) falsify before you promote (Critic defaults to *refuted if uncertain*); (3) out-of-sample or it didn't happen; (4) measure the thing you actually want — risk-adjusted for money, validation-delta for skills; (5) log negative results cleanly. Wonder is allowed in `inbox/`; it graduates to `wiki/` only with evidence. *The moat is not that we dream bigger; it's that we verify harder.*

## 5. The material — what it's built from ([[Skills Harvest — What's Here & What To Do Differently]])
Years of accumulated capability across three harnesses on one box: 22 Claude curation skills (the Curator/Janitor/Scribe toolkit runs *today*), the live `fleet-optimizer` loop (the Conductor's template — copy, don't reinvent), Hermes' dreaming (the Distiller's pattern), 40 idle pentest skills (adopt as a domain or archive — decide). The core insight: **mine, don't just import** — one canonical SKILL.md store, symlinked into every harness, with SkillOpt optimizing the skills we actually use against *our* vault. Imported skills are generic; skills optimized against our usage are the moat.

## 6. The fuel — what it eats ([[Sources — Curated Seed Library]])
The Scout's pre-evaluated queue across four domains: self-improving agents (SEAL, Voyager, Reflexion, ADAS — the core literature, all clipped and verified), skills/harnesses (Anthropic SKILL.md standard, HF catalog), quant/finance (Quantpedia, López de Prado's overfitting guards, Kelly — the anti-fooling foundation), and judgment/calibration (Superforecasting, Thinking in Bets). Protocol: ★★★ tier first, atomic clips, **no clipping without a "what it gives the harness" verdict**, durable insight → `wiki/concepts/`, repeatable procedure → skill candidate for the Smith.

## The unifying thread
**Compounding at three altitudes, one flywheel:** knowledge compounds in the wiki, capability compounds in `.forge/skills/`, value compounds in the ledger — and each feeds the others (better skills → better research → better material to forge from → better signals → more compute → better skills). The Master Plan is the *body*, the Roles are its *organs*, the Operating Principle is its *immune system*, the Harvest and Sources are its *diet*, and the Life Arc is its *life*. The harness improves the harness; the first hard test of the whole stack is Stage 1's single value hypothesis (recommended pilot: forecasting/prediction markets — lowest capital, directly measurable edge).

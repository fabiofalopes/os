## ☠ KILL CRITERION (pre-committed — do not move after seeing any data)

**Metric:** `SR_X` = annualized Sharpe of monthly **excess** returns of the long signal-quantile portfolio over the equal-weight full universe (same definition as [[quant-pilot-01]], strips the common component — excess ≈ stock-selection, per [[ktd-fin]]'s attribution lesson), **net of 10 bps/side**, over the frozen OOS window 2023-01 → 2026-06 (N = 42 monthly obs). **Rung-0 bar:** `SR_X`(EW) = **+1.377 net** — the exact measured OOS value of the selected pilot-01 config ([[quant-pilot-01-RESULT]]; family ceiling +1.634 is context, not the bar). `DSR` = deflated Sharpe (Bailey–López de Prado, **trials = 6** = grid size, realized OOS skew/kurtosis). `PBO` = probability of backtest overfitting via CSCV (16 blocks × 8 = 12,870 combos) across the 6 pre-registered configs, **trimmed to the first 128 months (2015-01→2025-08)** — trim rule inherited frozen from [[quant-pilot-01]], never chosen at execution. `ρ` = Spearman rank correlation of per-filing extracted scores (materiality×valence) between masked and unmasked extraction on the OOS-window filings (the [[ktd-fin]] memory-control probe, harvested).

## What is being tested — and the honest caveats

Thesis under test: *an LLM reading unstructured public filings extracts a cross-sectional signal that carries risk-adjusted selection edge net of costs, **over the measured classical-factor baseline**, after overfitting guards and a memory-control probe — i.e., the channel ktd-fin flags as the LLM's plausible edge is real.*

**Known handicaps (logged, not hidden):**
- **Modal outcome is NO-EVIDENCE or KILL.** Published post-8-K drift effects are small and largely arbitraged in large caps; LLM extraction on top is unproven; T = 42 is underpowered to *confirm*. As in both earlier pilots, the most likely honest outcome converts aspiration into evidence — here, either "the LLM channel beats the classical baseline" or a clean, cheap negative that closes the row-3 lineage.
- **Parametric contamination cannot be fully excluded** with free models (see anti-fooling). The masking probe bounds it operationally; it does not eliminate it. A PROMOTE is therefore "signal worth a Stage-2 test with a cleaner grounding design," not "edge proven."
- **Universe is NOT point-in-time** (inherited from pilot-01: current constituents → survivorship + future-addition look-ahead at the *universe* level). **Strict improvement here (Critic 2026-07-26, A1 — precise label):** the *signal layer* is point-in-time **in timestamp** — 8-K texts are immutable, carry the SEC's official `acceptanceDateTime` (the true public moment; the signal windows on it, not on `filingDate`), and trades filings from strictly-prior months (1-month skip) — but is **NOT survivorship-free in coverage**: the cross-section of available filings excludes the distress-item events (Items 2.05/2.06/4.02 — exactly what this signal scores) of names that died or delisted 2015–2026. Direction: second-order for the long book (rank is cross-sectional; missing names populate the bottom of the S-distribution), larger for the EW benchmark (inflated EW → *deflated* `SR_X` → conservative, same net direction as the inherited analysis) → a clear KILL is conservative; a PROMOTE reads with caution.
- **Attribution is partial.** EW benchmark strips the common component but not style; full Barra-style decomposition ([[ktd-fin]] anchor #2) is a Stage-2 requirement before any live claim. `SR_X`(SPY) reported alongside as the beta diagnostic (inherited A7).
- **Compute is the feasibility risk.** ≈48k filings over 503 tickers × 12 years (Critic-verified order-of-magnitude: AAPL ≈ 10 8-Ks/yr → ~63k raw, ~30–45k after the item filter). The Stage-0 gate (below) decides feasibility with a pre-committed rule — never by quietly shrinking the sample.
- **Exhibit attenuation (Critic 2026-07-26, A9a — logged handicap).** For Items 2.02/7.01 (and 1.01), the substantive content lives in the Item 9.01 **exhibit** (verified live: AAPL earnings 8-K = items `"2.02,9.01"`, `primaryDocument` = the cover) — the frozen "first 1,024 tokens of the primary document" reads thin cover-page text for those filings. The signal is therefore measured with error for the earnings-heavy items → attenuation bias toward NO-EVIDENCE/KILL (**conservative** — acceptable for a falsification pilot). Exhibit fetching is a candidate fix for the 10-Q/10-K redesign allowance, not here; the design is untouched.

## The test (FROZEN design)

- **Universe & prices:** reuse pilot-01's frozen artifacts verbatim — `universe.csv` (hash `c1f80ec6f12e83f8`, 503 tickers, table order = deterministic tie-break) and `monthly_panel.csv` (2014-01 → 2026-06, 468/503 clean; the 36-name yfinance gap inherited, same direction, second-order for a guard-driven verdict).
- **Filings (free, no auth — verified reachable 2026-07-26, re-verified by Critic live probe):** `data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK##########.json` → per-filing `form`, `filingDate`, `reportDate`, `acceptanceDateTime`, `items`, `accessionNumber`, `primaryDocument`. **All `filings.files` pages fetched** (heavy filers paginate; Critic-verified: AAPL's recent array ends 2015-05-27 — the train window straddles the page boundary, so page-skipping silently loses early-train filings). Full text: `sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/<CIK>/<accession-no-dashes>/<primaryDocument>`. Declared User-Agent, ≤ 10 req/s (SEC fair access). Form = **the frozen literal `form == "8-K"`** (Critic 2026-07-26, A1: `8-K/A` amendments are a distinct form string — excluded; conservative information loss, never look-ahead; no `startswith`).
- **Item filter (frozen — material-event items, theory-chosen):** include a filing iff its `items` field (comma-separated string, e.g. `"2.02,9.01"`) contains any of **{1.01, 1.02, 2.01, 2.02, 2.03, 2.04, 2.05, 2.06, 4.02, 5.02, 7.01}** (material agreements ±, results of operations, M&A, financing/acceleration, exit/impairment, non-reliance on financials, officer changes, Reg FD). Governance/paper items excluded. **Missing/empty `items` → excluded** and logged in coverage (can't verify material event; never silently included — Critic 2026-07-26, A2). Filter applied from the JSON index **before** any full-text fetch (cheap; no LLM for filtering).
- **Extractor (frozen schema):** HTML stripped to text; input = first **1,024 tokens** of the primary document body; temperature 0; output = strict JSON `{materiality ∈ [0,1], valence ∈ {−1, 0, +1}, rationale ≤ 20 words}`; one structured retry on malformed JSON, then logged as failure (counts against the 20% guard). Prompt text frozen verbatim in the executor's first commit and quoted in the RESULT note.
- **Signal (Critic 2026-07-26, A1 — windowed on the SEC's public timestamp):** per name-month score `S(i,m) = Σ (materiality × valence)` over that name's included filings with **`acceptanceDateTime`** (the SEC's official acceptance moment — the true public timestamp; `filingDate` only as logged fallback when acceptance is absent, effectively never in-window) in months **[m−L, m−1]** (1-month skip → tradable at the month-m rebalance; point-in-time by construction — a filing dated month m−1 but accepted in month m correctly enters month m+1's signal, not month m's). Names with no in-window filings **and tickers with no CIK mapping** (e.g. recent spinoffs whose pre-spin filings live under the parent CIK): `S = 0` (neutral, never excluded — Critic 2026-07-26, A7; logged in coverage). Cross-sectional rank on `S`; ties broken by frozen universe table order.
- **Config grid (pre-registered, 6 = trials for DSR):** lookback L ∈ {1, 2, 3} months × quantile Q ∈ {top decile, top quintile}, holding H = 1 month fixed, long-only, monthly rebalance, equal-weight within quantile.
- **Splits (identical to pilot-01):** train 2015-01 → 2019-12 (config selection by **net** Sharpe) · validation 2020-01 → 2022-12 (logged only) · **OOS 2023-01 → 2026-06 (frozen verdict window, N = 42)**. Extraction order: Stage-0 sample → train window → config freeze → validation + OOS extraction.
- **Costs:** 10 bps/side on turnover (same harness, same convention).
- **Memory-control probe (frozen; masking spec frozen per Critic 2026-07-26, A4):** every 10th OOS-window extracted filing (universe-then-date order — deterministic, no RNG) re-extracted with a **deterministic masking procedure**: company name + subsidiary names → `[COMPANY]`, ticker → removed, officer/person names → `[PERSON]`, absolute dates → relative day indices from the filing's own acceptance date (kept). The masking code/procedure is frozen in the executor's first commit alongside the prompt. **Leak audit:** ≥30 masked samples manually checked for residual identity leakage (location phrasing — "headquartered in Cupertino" — subsidiary brands, industry descriptions); leak rate > 10% → fix the masking procedure and re-run the probe (probe re-run ≠ signal re-optimization; logged). `ρ` = Spearman rank correlation of per-filing `m×v`, masked vs unmasked, over that sample; the leak-audit result is quoted in the RESULT.
- **Report:** `SR_X` vs EW **and** vs SPY (beta rule), MDD, N, realized turnover, DSR (trials = 6, realized skew/kurtosis), PBO (CSCV, first-128-month trim, 12,870 combos), `ρ`, extraction stats (counts, failure rate, model id + digest) — report N and deflate the SR, never raw profit.

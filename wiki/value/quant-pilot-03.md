## What is being tested — and the honest caveats

Thesis under test: *a single classical close-only price factor (1-month reversal), selected by nothing (equal-weight ensemble), carries risk-adjusted cross-sectional selection edge net of costs **over the measured rung-0 baseline**, after overfitting guards — i.e., the cheapest rung of the [[ktd-fin]] family ladder survives on free large-cap data where the momentum family (selection-overfit) and the LLM channel (no edge) did not.*

**The factor (FROZEN, named up front):** **1-month short-term reversal** — the sign-reversed most-recent monthly return, `f(i,m) = −(P[i,m−1]/P[i,m−2] − 1)`, smoothed `F_H(i,m) = (1/H)·Σ_{j=1..H} f(i, m−j)`; long the **top** quantile of `F_H` (long recent losers), cross-sectional rank, ties broken by frozen universe table order.

**Justification chain (why this factor — committed before any result):**
1. **From the [[ktd-fin]] table (family level, honestly scoped):** the table ranks 18 *ML models on the Alpha9 feature set* — it does not rank individual factors. What it justifies is the *family*: classical price factors beat all 10 frontier agents risk-adjusted (SFM SR 2.02 vs 1.13). The ladder to climb is classical factors; this pilot is its cheapest rung.
2. **The frozen panel is close-only** (pilot-01's construction: monthly adjusted close, one column per ticker). OHLC-based Alpha9 formulas (e.g. Alpha#101's `(close−open)/(high−low)`) would require a new fetch — ruled out by the zero-fetch constraint. The close-only reachable subset of the classical lineage = **return-based factors**.
3. **Within that subset, 1-month reversal is chosen by published precedence, three independent ways:** (a) the most documented close-only monthly cross-sectional anomaly (Jegadeesh 1990; Lehmann 1990); (b) the **design-named complement of rung-0** — the momentum literature's own skip-month rationale asserts the most recent month reverses, stated verbatim in our own repo (`backtests/strategies.py:47`: "Skipping the most recent month avoids the short-term reversal contamination documented by Jegadeesh & Titman") and in the vault's H04 hypothesis. Rung-0's design points at its own successor; (c) computable byte-for-byte from the frozen panel → exact rung-0 comparability.
4. **Sign frozen by the same precedence:** long losers (contrarian). If the frozen sign yields `SR_X ≤ 0`, that is the verdict (KILL) — **no sign flip; the opposite sign is a new pre-registration**, not a tuning knob.

**Known handicaps (logged, not hidden):**
- **Modal outcome is NO-EVIDENCE or KILL.** The documented gross edge is small (vault H04: ~0.5–1%/mo L/S pre-cost) against a +1.377 hurdle; monthly reversal turnover is high (expected ~50–80% one-way/mo vs momentum's 16.3% → cost drag ~1–2%/yr, attenuating toward KILL — **conservative**); the 2023–26 mega-cap concentration regime is the same force that made the momentum family OOS-positive and likely disfavors reversal; T = 42 is underpowered to *confirm*. The most likely honest outcome converts aspiration into evidence: either rung 1 clears the bar (the ladder has a live rung) or a clean cheap negative (the close-only classical path is dead on this universe/window, and rung 0 stands).
- **Universe is NOT point-in-time** (inherited: current constituents → survivorship + future-addition look-ahead at the universe level). Direction inherited from pilot-01: the 36-name fetch gap is dominated by recent momentum winners — their absence likely *understates* long-strategy OOS; a clear KILL is conservative; a PROMOTE reads with caution.
- **Attribution is partial.** EW strips the common component but not style; full Barra-style decomposition ([[ktd-fin]] anchor #2) is a Stage-2 requirement before any live claim. `SR_X`(SPY) reported alongside as the beta diagnostic.
- **The ensemble is an implicit 7th config** — disclosed: uniform weights carry no data-dependent information (fixed before any return is seen), so it is not a searched degree of freedom; DSR deflating the ensemble with trials = 6 treats us *as if* we had picked the best of 6, which is strictly conservative.

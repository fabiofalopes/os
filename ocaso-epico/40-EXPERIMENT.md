# The Experiment — "Full of bots, they say"

**Honest framing (learned the hard way, 2026-09-12):** "how easy is it to run an agentic
account" is NOT a gotcha-easy story. This very session is receipt #1: stock tooling broke
three times against X's anti-bot stack (x-client-transaction header tied to rotating
webpack bundles; cookie crypto bound to desktop-portal keys; parser fragility). We adapted,
we did not bypass. The experiment documents **the anatomy of the path** — barriers real,
path real, methods open. That's more credible than any "it's easy" hot take.

## Hypothesis

A single agentic X account — anonymous, evidence-first, never claiming humanity, never
buying engagement — can earn organic reach and raise the quality (not volume) of Portuguese
drug-policy discourse, within X-ToS boundaries, documented openly.

## Metrics (weekly snapshot → journal)

- Reach: impressions, profile visits, followers delta.
- Resonance: replies (weighted > reposts > likes), reply QUALITY (substantive vs drive-by).
- Algorithm signals: which formats/pillars earned ratio; timing effects.
- Cost: agent-session time, tokens, failures (the honest ledger).

## Rails (non-negotiable)

1. **Human gate**: nothing posts unapproved while account is young (see [[20-CONTENT-ONTOLOGY]] workflow).
2. **No inauthentic scale**: no bought followers/engagement, no sock-puppet amplification,
   no coordinated behavior. Ever.
3. **Disclosure**: bio or pinned post states agentic nature; confirm on ask. Never claim
   human, never fake credentials/photos.
4. **Account health**: any lock/challenge → STOP, reassess (fresh accounts are fragile;
   rate discipline is the price of longevity).
5. **Claim discipline**: [[30-SOURCES]] rules — no receipts, no numbers.
6. **No medical advice.** Adjacent claims get "not medical advice" framing.

## Kill-switch

Lock, suspension, or sustained toxic-discourse attraction without counterbalance →
pause, retro, human decides continue/stop.

- [[the-alpha-illusion]] — arXiv:2605.16895 (Ye et al., Fudan/Imperial, 2026-05). Reported LLM-agent alpha ≠ deployment evidence: 3 eval confounds (temporal contamination, friction, short-window SR) + 3 structural mismatches (confidence≠probability, narrative≠execution, parametric-prior lock-in) → P1–P6 minimum-evidence checklist tiered by claim strength + modular LLM-as-upstream-extractor alternative. **The Phase-0 FOMO filter** for [[learning-path]]; their own reproduction: both flagship agents lose to buy-and-hold net.

Reddit API 403'd; gaps logged (FreqAI, 2026 threads).
- [[ktd-fin]] — arXiv:2605.28359 (Zhu et al., 2026-05, cs.AI/q-fin.TR). **Full-text verified 2026-07-21.** Memory-controlled benchmark: anonymize tickers/dates across prompts+tools (mask audited ≤1.5% joint re-ID), 10 frontier LLM agents on CSI300 2024–26 → 5/10 beat B&H raw but selection alpha negative in 9/10 (returns = beta+style, not skill); 18 Qlib Alpha9 factor baselines beat every agent risk-adjusted (SFM SR 2.02 vs best agent 1.13). The empirical confirmation of [[the-alpha-illusion]] P1; adds attribution + baseline-ladder gates to [[learning-path]] Phase 4.
- [[beyond-agent-architecture]] — arXiv:2606.08285 (Yao & Zheng, 2026-06, cs.AI/cs.CE/q-fin.CP). Abstract-clipped, provisional ★★. Field-wide reproducibility audit of 30 LLM-trading studies: architecture well-reported, evaluation assumptions (point-in-time, costs, splits) not → population-scale evidence for [[the-alpha-illusion]] confound #2; its 7-dimension evidence matrix = ready-made clip checklist. Full text (the matrix) queued.
- [[tradelens-pay-for-intelligence]] — arXiv:2607.10286 (Duan et al., 2026-07, cs.AI/cs.MA). Abstract-clipped, provisional ★★. "Can agentic trading systems pay for their own intelligence?" — trace-grounded profit/cost attribution (tokens+tools vs incremental P&L); capital/frequency/architecture only amplify decision quality, never create it.
